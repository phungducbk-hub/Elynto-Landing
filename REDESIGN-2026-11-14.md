## Final experience
- Still Harbor #1a6289, Inter, canvas #f7f7f8, text #1a2530 and border #dbe0e6 across Vietnamese/English pages.
- All preview UI follows the selected page language, including sidebar, Today, work cards, tables, statuses, planner and project views. Shared vocabulary lives in src/content/product-ui.ts.
- The hero demo starts when the actual product viewport becomes at least half visible, restarts from typing on re-entry, and pauses offscreen or in a hidden browser tab. The CTA is optional. Reduced-motion visitors also get the automatic content sequence, with gentler presentation.
- The request is revealed one Unicode character every 60ms, including with reduced-motion preferences. Vietnamese starts with “Giao Minh làm báo giá, hoàn thành trước thứ sáu.”; English uses the matching quotation request.
- Work-card and work-table due dates use fixed sample dates (20/11/2026, 18/11/2026 and 15/11/2026) in both locales. Natural-language requests retain Friday / next Wednesday / tomorrow.
- Sequence: type a sample request → send → skeleton loading → work card with title, assignee, due date, priority and status → My Work with one highlighted new row. The work card stays for five seconds and also has a button to open the work list immediately.
- Three requests, Send, pause/play, replay and tab navigation are available. Creation occurs only in browser-local sample state, without an AI/backend request or a beta write. The card is a landing demonstration, not a claim that a live production command was recorded.
- Skeleton loading adapts the supplied reference to the existing framer-motion dependency.
- The original Sound familiar / Nghe có quen không content and card design are retained. Columns automatically scroll at different speeds when the wall enters view and pause offscreen. No card-wall Play/Resume button remains; hovering does not stop the tracks. This requested autoplay also runs under reduced-motion preferences.
- Product tables and demo frames enter gently from their side or from below as they scroll into view. Latest requested tuning: 4.8s slide with symmetric gentle acceleration/deceleration, 0.9s fade so most travel remains visible, 96px sideways on desktop or 40-72px from below, without scaling. Product visuals use these same values under reduced-motion preferences because the user explicitly requested longer, slower travel; unrelated text keeps reduced-motion treatment. A slow-hydration fallback keeps visible content readable while re-arming below-fold entrances.
- User-facing punctuation and browser tab/social titles consistently use short hyphens (-).
- You / your team continues to alternate every 2.8 seconds, with no rolling transition when reduced motion is requested.
- Feature previews use the inspected app layout; List / Calendar / Gantt retain the sample dataset and prior supplied beta references. Removed the unverified 10-second planning claim. Today’s sample briefing is not described as real reporting.
